# Mode · deep

Create detailed, learnable lecture notes that follow the actual classroom sequence.

- **Preserve the actual lecture order. Do not reorganize by a new knowledge structure.** Keep concepts, derivations, examples, comparisons, conditions, questions, corrections and course requirements where the teacher discusses them. If the teacher starts with course rules, the body starts there too; the upfront assessment digest does not replace that discussion.
- Deepen the explanation within each local passage, clarifying its reasoning and connections without moving later material forward or merging distant passages. Preserve substance and use natural paragraphs across adjacent ASR segments.
- Explain course reasoning clearly. Any background explanation, new example, assumption, or derivation not present in the course must be explicitly marked as supplemental.
- Do not silently replace the teacher's framing with external/common-knowledge framing.
- Use formulas, tables, or Mermaid only when they improve understanding; avoid decorative diagrams and oversized tables.
- Length should follow the material rather than a fixed chapter/word-count template.
