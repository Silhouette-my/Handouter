# Slides · embed

PPT may be used as evidence and selected images may be embedded in the final Markdown.

- Embed only slides that materially help the nearby explanation; do not dump every adjacent slide.
- Use real copied images only. For notes under `notes/`, slide links normally use `../slides/images/<filename>`.
- Keep captions concise and faithful to the course material.
- `slides/alignment.json` is temporal overlap, not semantic proof. Verify relevance before embedding.
- Preserve teacher revisits and unknown/missing slide states rather than forcing a fabricated page sequence.
