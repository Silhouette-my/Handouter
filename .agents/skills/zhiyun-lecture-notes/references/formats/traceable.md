# Format · traceable

Keep the note readable while making detailed provenance available on expansion.

- Follow common/presentation: folded course information and one closed `本章时间与来源` HTML details block after each major section (`##`) heading.
- Compared with clean, include fuller segment/PPT occurrence mappings and the evidence used for corrections inside the folds. Keep the summary label free of timestamps and source IDs.
- Aggregate paragraph, list and table sources in their parent chapter's single fold; do not add per-paragraph, per-item or per-row folds. Preserve separate intervals when summary regroups distant passages.
- Provenance should point to real available evidence only; unknown timing stays unknown.
- Internal implementation details and private paths are still not reader content.
- Keep the final unresolved checklist and its fuller evidence inside one closed `待核对` fold, not a visible `## 待核对` chapter.
